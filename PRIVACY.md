# Privacy

ScaleLauncher is designed for local processing and does not require an Internet connection.

## Network access

ScaleLauncher does not request the Android INTERNET permission and therefore cannot transmit data over the Internet itself.

Communication with the Xiaomi Body Composition Scale S400 happens directly over Bluetooth.

Paired ScaleLauncher devices can exchange selected profile and measurement data directly over encrypted Bluetooth connections.

## Measurement and profile data

Measurements, user profiles, configuration and the scale login token are processed and/or stored locally on the respective Android device.

ScaleLauncher does not send this data to its own servers or cloud services.

## openScale

When openScale is used, ScaleLauncher passes measurements locally through the Android provider interface exposed by openScale.

## Health Connect

Health Connect is optional.

When enabled, ScaleLauncher writes only the measurement values selected by the user through the local Android Health Connect interface.

ScaleLauncher does not read health data from Health Connect.

## Analytics, tracking and advertising

ScaleLauncher contains:

- no advertising
- no analytics services
- no tracking SDKs
- no telemetry
- no crash-reporting services

## Third parties

ScaleLauncher does not use Xiaomi cloud services for normal operation.

The app is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with Xiaomi, openScale or Google.
